Harissa Prawns

Ingredients List

  • 75g Prawns
  • 40g Garlic Butter
  • 10g Harissa Paste
  • Parsley for Garnish

Instructions

  1. In a Bowl add the Prawns, Harissa Paste and Garlic Butter
  2. Heat until the butter is melted and the prawns are at a safe temperature (75c minimum)
  3. Garnish with Parsley and serve with Garlic Bread
